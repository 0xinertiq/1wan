Daily Intel Brief, Sat 14 Nov 2026 SGT

Read-through: A December Fed hike is now close to fully priced, with the 10-year above 5.1%, and the chip complex and bitcoin both sold off into it. Yen near 160 adds Tokyo intervention risk. Oil is the one pressure valve, easing on lower odds of renewed US strikes on Iran. Nvidia earnings Tuesday and Anthropic's pricing Wednesday are the next tests.

## Top 5 headlines

**A December Fed hike is priced at 100% after October PPI rose 0.3% on the month, with the 10-year at about 5.14% and the 30-year at 5.47%.** The monthly print was cooler than the 0.4% expected, but annual PPI is 6.5%, the highest since November 2022, and core rose 0.5% by one economist's count; Waller said the print alone does not rule out a 50bp hike on 9 December. Rising real yields drain liquidity from long-duration assets, and the 10-year's 5.17% intraday high is the level to watch. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/11/13/treasury-yields-fed-rates.html), [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/equities/ppi-still-uncomfortably-high-as-december-rate-hike-looms/269002)

**Bitcoin broke below $70,000 for the first time since August and sat near $68,200 on Crypto.com's perpetual at 23:53 UTC, with Ether near $1,926.** Bitcoin is down more than 10% in November, ETF outflows have run for six straight sessions, and liquidations were put at $1.4 to $1.6 billion, almost 90% longs, though outlets disagree on the totals. The marginal buyer is a rate-sensitive allocator, so ETF flow turning flat is the first sign of a floor, and the 13 Nov intraday low near $67,400 is the nearest level in play. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-november-13-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-nvidia-ppi-141451558.html), [Coinpaper](https://coinpaper.com/38802/bitcoin-et-fs-bleed-for-sixth-straight-day-as-btc-falls-nearly-5)

**Chips sold off hard after Nvidia's CEO exit: the Nasdaq closed down 0.91% at 25,136, the S&P 500 down 0.40% at 7,562, and AMD fell about 9%.** Nvidia dropped as much as 14% at the open before closing down about 4% on Yahoo's count, though other outlets show 2 to 3.5%, and HSBC cut it to Hold with a $170 target. Asia's chip-heavy indices carry the same key-person discount into Monday, and Nvidia's results on Tuesday 17 November are the hard catalyst. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-november-13-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-nvidia-ppi-141451558.html), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-nov-13-2026)

**The yen is at 159.71 per dollar per TradingView after Prime Minister Takaichi and Finance Minister Katayama both warned on Friday about intervention.** Japan bought yen in late July alongside a US rate check, and the pair returned to 160 within three months, so the warning is credible but the effect has been short-lived. A sudden yen rally would pressure exporters and unwind carry positions, and Japan's 10-year at 2.86% shows the other side of that trade. — [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/japanese-yen-ticks-up-from-16000-as-pm-takaichi-warns-of-intervention-202611130736), [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/markets/currencies/japan-carries-out-yen-buying-intervention-as-us-executes-rate-check)

**Brent settled near $95.8 and is set for a second weekly decline as Trump eased fears of renewed war with Iran.** Hormuz status is unsettled: one tracker says commercial traffic remains minimal, another says flows have largely recovered, and Iran's mine claims against a tanker are unconfirmed. Oil has fallen about 20% from the $118 high of 30 October, so the market is already pricing de-escalation and the risk is a headline reversal. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/11/13/treasury-yields-fed-rates.html), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/11/13/oil-on-track-to-post-second-weekly-decline-as-trump-eases-fears-of-renewed-war-with-iran/)

No ceasefire is agreed, so energy and defence pricing keep trading on headlines, and Zelenskyy's response is the next thing to watch. — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/11/12/nx-s1-6038809/us-envoys-meet-putin-russia-ukraine-war-talks), [Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/world/article-us-envoys-meet-putin-ukraine-peace-talks/)
