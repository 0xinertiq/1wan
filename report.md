Daily Intel Brief, Sat 28 Nov 2026 SGT

Wall Street closed its best week since April 2025 even as the 10-year yield pushed to 4.94%, so equities are rallying into tighter money while bitcoin sits below $60,000 under record ETF outflows. Hormuz remains the oil swing factor, and a December Fed hike is being discussed as near-certain.

## Top 5 headlines

**US stocks closed Friday's half session higher: the S&P 500 rose 1.6% to 7,297.49, the Nasdaq 2.3% to 24,129.50 and the Dow 1.2%, for a 5.6% weekly gain, while the 10-year yield rose to 4.94% from 4.85%.** Equities are rising into higher real yields, with energy-driven inflation worsening in October, so the squeeze falls on anything that depends on easy liquidity. The 5% handle on the 10-year is the level to watch. — [AP via Post-Gazette](https://post-gazette.com/business/personal-finance/stock-markets-today-november-27-2026-2KMWD23HNN7BAM2FQEDY3KOQKU)

**Bitcoin is holding near $59,850 on the Crypto.com perpetual at 23:53 UTC, with a 24-hour range of $58,715 to $62,150, and ETH is near $1,660.** Spot bitcoin ETFs have lost a record $5.39 billion in November through Wednesday, seven straight outflow sessions, so the bid that carried earlier rallies is missing. Friday's $6.8 billion Deribit expiry had a max pain of $70,000 per CryptoQuant, which is far above spot, and $58,700 is the low to watch. — [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/bitcoin-price-rebounds-68b-options-expiry-november-27/)

**Iran's IRGC says a tanker hit a mine and exploded in the Strait of Hormuz, and an Italian live blog reports only two vessels transiting in 24 hours with about 400 ships waiting.** The transit figure rests on a single report and is undated, but it fits the picture of a strait that is effectively shut, which keeps an energy risk premium inside inflation and yields. Brent has ranged between $72 and $114 since February per AP. — [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/11/27/iran-war-live-irans-araghchi-calls-deadly-kuwait-attack-suspicious?update=5191820), [Il Sole 24 Ore](https://en.ilsole24ore.com/art/war-latest-news-al-jazeera-israeli-drone-strikes-police-station-in-gaza-three-dead-AJlV8zQC)

**Commentators are treating a quarter-point Fed hike on 9 December as close to certain, and the RBA has already raised five times.** That comment comes from one economist quoted in a live blog, so confirm against futures pricing. A hiking Fed with oil elevated is a direct drag on crypto liquidity, and the inflation print and Fed speakers before 9 December are the dates to watch. — [ABC News](https://abc.net.au/news/2026-11-27/asx-markets-business-news-live-updates/107430390)

**Asian stocks extended their rebound on Friday: the Nikkei was up about 2% and the Hang Seng about 1.8% in intraday reports, with Kospi reports ranging widely.** Figures differ by time of day and outlet, so treat them as approximate. This is a bounce after Wednesday's chip-led rout rather than a trend change. — [ABC News](https://abc.net.au/news/2026-11-27/asx-markets-business-news-live-updates/107430390)
