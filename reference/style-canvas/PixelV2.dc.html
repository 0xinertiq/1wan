What I took from your Bao reference
Warm paper and an amber wash behind the characters, with grain.
Blush cheeks and tiny simple faces: dot eyes, small mouths.
Brush lettering and a red seal for the Chinese ink feel.
Soft, round, slightly squashed shapes like Coco's bun body.
I couldn't open the cat-food mascot link from here. Send a screenshot or two and I'll fold its style in.
Visual upgrades in this version Three-tone shading and a highlight on every character. Idle animation: bobbing, blinks, tails that sway, imp wings that flap, a green candle that "pumps," Coco's cheeks puffing. One light direction, whole-pixel movement only.
Still placeholder, for the artist Drawn at 32 px by code, so it's simpler than real sprites. A pixel artist would redraw at 48 px, add more frames (walk, happy, sad, level-up), and tune the palette and anti-aliasing. Colours and shapes here are the brief, not the final art.
