Voice rulesShort and kind. Never shames a loss. Never says buy or sell. Quotes appear after a miss, praise after a kept rule. No emoji: use pixel icons. Each crew member has one tic: Kumo says "Queen", Tilly says "wait", Momo says "first", Dundun counts risk in percent, Jin ends on a price, Nini ends on a breath.
Behaviour rulesType out one character at a time with a soft blip, tap to finish. Name tag always carries the speaker's colour. Tail points to the speaker. Max two bubbles on screen. Warnings can be muted per villain; Dundun cannot be muted, only quieted. Respect reduced motion: bubbles fade in instead of typing.

Interactions
Tap
Talk to a character; tap again to skip the typing.
Hold
Open their room: Fenn the scan, Coco the ledger, Sisi the library.
Drag
Drag a receipt to Coco, a coin to Jin, a quote to Sisi.
Swipe
Glide the camera between Market, Body, Mind and Craft.
Pull the bell
Pull down to ring the bell; Kumo gives the day summary.
Long idle
Crew nap, tail sway slows, lanterns dim. Opening the app wakes them.
Streak broken
Kintsugi: a gold crack on the dais, not a punishment. One tap repairs it.
Rank up
Court freezes, Kumo bows, new frame drops on the card. Once only.
Build note: the court is one wide 2D scene, a camera and sprites, so it is cheap to build and works on phone and desktop. No 3D, no physics. Everything above uses only the characters and parts already drawn.
