Do they still pop as a profile picture?
At profile-picture size only the head and shoulders show, so the big levers sit up there: hair shape and colour, ears, horns, halos, headwear, plus a background colour and frame. Rank shows on the card as a badge and frame, so everything on the character is free to be personal.
The levers that make people look different
1. Start with a face and body. Five skin tones, several faces, hair shapes, body types. Everyone begins unique.
2. Ancestry. Human, fox spirit, cat, moon rabbit, dragon-kin. Ears, tails and horns change the silhouette.
3. Big back and head pieces. Wings, tails, halos, horns, crowns. These are what you see from far away.
4. Dyes. Any item can be recoloured in 2 to 3 channels. This alone multiplies the looks hugely and sells well.
5. Held props and companions. Fan, umbrella, sword, lantern, a pet.
6. Animated effects. Sparkles, auras, trails, idle emotes.
7. Card scenery. Backgrounds, frames, stickers, a title line.
