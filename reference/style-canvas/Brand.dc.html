Why a seal: a red chop is how a Chinese work is signed, so it is the one mark that sits on every surface (logo, PFP, cards, export). It is square, pixel-notched and always tilted 6 degrees left, except in the app icon. The name is read "wan", from 完整, and the brackets are part of the wordmark. Check before launch: domain, X and Instagram handles, trademark search.

4 · Character kit · what makes anything "1(WAN)"
1
Gold mark. Every character carries one gold accent: Kumo's forehead, Dundun's mane, Jin's body, Sisi's scroll ends, Nini's crown.
2
Blush cheeks. Two pink pixels on every face, even villains (tinted coral). Warmth is the family trait.
3
Dot eyes. Two tall dark pixels, one white highlight pixel on the upper left. Villains get yellow eyes and brows.
4
Plum outline. One unit of plum around the union of all parts, never black, never inside the shape.
5
Cream tip. Tails, ears and feet end in cream. It is what makes the silhouette read at 32 px.
6
Three tones. Base, shade on the right and below, light on the upper left. No gradients, no anti-aliasing.
7
Inner ear. Ears get a darker inner pixel. Pointed ears for foxes and cats, round for hamster and lion.
Canvas
32 by 32 units. Humans 32 by 38. Everything snaps to whole units.
Scale
Show at 4x, 6x or 8x so a pixel is always a whole number of screen pixels.
Light
From the upper left, always. Shade falls right and down.
Props
Held items on the right hand, companions on the right. Capes and tails on the left and back.
Animation
Two frames: a 1 unit bob every 1.4s and a blink every 3.8s. Steps, no easing. Tails sway in 3 frames.
Never
Soft shadows, outlines in other colours, glossy highlights, 3D, gradients, real photos.

7 · Motion, sound and voice
Motion
  • Frame-stepped only: steps(1,end), no easing.
  • Idle bob 1.4s, blink 3.8s, tail sway 3 frames.
  • Screen changes are a 4-frame wipe in plum, not a fade.
  • Rewards pop up 8 px, hold 0.6s, then rest.
  • Alerts shake the frame once. Reduced motion shows a still frame and a border.
Sound
Chiptune on a pentatonic scale, with a guqin-style pluck for the home theme. Off by default on first open; one tap turns it on.
Tapsoft blip
Rite donefive rising notes
Rank upgong, then three notes
Warninglow double pulse
Nightone soft pluck every minute
Voice
  • Warm, brief, a little dry. Like an older sister who keeps your promises.
  • Praise the process ("rule kept"), never the profit.
  • After a loss: a quote, a breath, a next step. Never "you should have".
  • No buy or sell calls, no emoji, no exclamation marks except villains.
  • Chinese appears as a label (精气神, 寒露), never as a translation puzzle.
8 · Do and don't
Do Put the seal on every PFP, card and export.
Don't Recolour or redraw the seal.
Do Give new characters a gold accent and blush cheeks.
Don't Add a character with a black outline or realistic shading.
Do Use paper grain behind pixel art.
Don't Put pixel art on flat white or neon gradients.
Do Earn rank pips; buy dyes and costumes.
Don't Sell rank, crowns or the rank frame.
Do Keep PFP marks inside the circle crop.
Don't Place the seal or pip in the corners.
Do Use the pixel face for names and the brush face for Chinese.
Don't Mix a fourth typeface in.
